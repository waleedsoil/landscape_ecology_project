How it works

TerrainThe course’s 1 m DGM, resampled to 3 m. Heights are exaggerated 1.5× in the view only; slope, aspect and the horizon of each plot are calculated at true scale.
PlotsField positions measured by GPS on 21 Nov. Moved plots are kept in this browser; use Copy coordinates and paste the numbers into PLOTS_UTM in assets/js/simulator.js so everyone sees them.
SunSun position from the NOAA solar equations for 51.560° N, 9.965° E, CET, in 10-minute steps through the logging period. Play runs through the days; the dashed arc is the sun path on 3 Dec.
My locationShows where you are on the knoll, using your phone’s GPS. The browser asks for permission first; the position stays in your browser and is not sent anywhere. Under trees GPS is often off by 5–20 m, so the blue circle shows the accuracy the phone reports. Press again to stop.
Direct sunZero when the sun is below the plot’s terrain horizon or behind its own slope; otherwise the share of the beam that hits the slope head-on. Clouds and the canopy cut it further, and that is what the loggers will measure.
